# Prior-Art and Claim Boundary

## What the targeted search found

A dedicated prior-art investigation searched for equivalents of the H6 lower bound under several languages, including:

- quadratic maps over (mathbb F_2);
- vectorial Boolean functions;
- degree-constrained polynomial images;
- Reed–Muller evaluation/interpolation;
- information sets and evaluation geometry;
- related finite-geometric and coding-theoretic formulations.

No equivalent result establishing the same universal 24-target containment statement, or equivalently (h(6)ge25) under the frozen definition, was located in that targeted search.

## What that means

The defensible public statement is:

> **H6 is a CIEC LAB mathematical discovery for which no equivalent result was found in the targeted prior-art search.**

## What it does not mean

The search is not a proof that no mathematically equivalent statement has ever appeared in every paper, thesis, database, archive or unpublished source.

Accordingly this repository does not claim an exhaustive first-in-history priority certification.

Correctness and originality are separate questions:

- the frozen proof status is documented in this repository;
- the targeted prior-art status is “no equivalent found”;
- global historical priority is not used as a premise of the theorem.

The standard ([14,3,8]) duplicated-simplex object encountered in related H6 work is known prior art; no novelty claim is made for that object.
