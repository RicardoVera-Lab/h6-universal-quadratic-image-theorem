## Description / abstract
Let h(n) be the least cardinality of a set H⊂F₂^(2n) that is not contained in the image of any map q:F₂^n→F₂^(2n) whose coordinate functions have algebraic degree at most two. We prove, by a combination of human-readable reductions and exact finite certificates, that h(6)≥25. Equivalently, every set of at most 24 points in F₂^12 is contained in the image of some coordinatewise quadratic map F₂^6→F₂^12. The proof factors every such map through the 22-dimensional degree-≤2 evaluation embedding and reduces the problem to adaptive matching of dependency spaces. The finite certificate layer consists of 46 dangerous two-dimensional weight profiles, 45 exact-kernel witness pairs, nine exact rational Delsarte/Krawtchouk certificates, and an exhaustive MacWilliams feasibility closure, with a separate all-one exceptional branch. All proof-authoritative computation uses exact integer or rational arithmetic. A public verifier and witness corpus are supplied for reproduction.
