# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Public by design

This repository exposes the material needed to evaluate H6 as a public research artifact:

- exact theorem statement;
- evaluation-geometry reformulation;
- proof from first principles;
- conceptual explanation of the 22-to-24 gain;
- finite certificate architecture;
- adversarial attack record;
- external mathematical dependency;
- residual risks;
- public clean-room verifier;
- exact-kernel witness corpus;
- reproducibility instructions;
- targeted prior-art status.

## Proprietary by design

CIEC LAB's internal research operating architecture, orchestration rules, hidden prompts, agent control logic and broader laboratory memory are proprietary and are not required to evaluate the public theorem.

A second internal checker is documented as an independence control; the public artifact is not intended to reproduce the laboratory itself.

## Principle

> **Public evidence. Private machinery.**

The objective is to make the claim auditable without making CIEC LAB reproducible as an operating system.
